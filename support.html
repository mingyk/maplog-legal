How it works

  • Tap the camera button and take a photo — it's placed on the map where you took it.
  • Add a caption, tags, or @mention friends, then post.
  • Your photos automatically connect into the path of your day.
  • Only friends you've added (and who've added you back) can see your map.
  • Share a path or your daily recap out to Instagram or your camera roll.

Frequently asked questions

My photo isn't on the map.

maplog needs location to place a photo where you took it. Make sure location permission is enabled in your device Settings → maplog → Location (set to "While Using the App").

Where did my photos go?

maplog is ephemeral by design. Your shots and paths are automatically and permanently deleted a couple of days after you post them. If you want to keep something, share it out before it expires. The day resets at 4 AM local time, and the previous day becomes a recap.

How do I add friends?

Open the Friends screen to send and accept requests, or use "Invite friends" to share a link. You'll only see someone's map once you've added each other.

Location permission

maplog only uses location while you're using the app, to place your photos and connect your path — there is no background tracking. You can change this anytime in your device Settings → maplog → Location.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to the Profile tab, tap the gear (Settings) icon, scroll to Danger Zone, and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data. You can also email us to request deletion.

How is my data handled?

Your location and content are used only to run the app, are visible only to your friends, and are never sold or used for ads. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
