How to play

  • Tap Claim turf and start moving on foot — walking or running.
  • Loop back to where you started to close the shape.
  • The area inside your loop becomes your territory, shown in your color on the map.
  • Run over someone's land to take it; they can take yours the same way.
  • Re-run your own ground to reinforce it with a shield.

Frequently asked questions

Why won't my run count?

TURF is on-foot only. Movement faster than running speed (for example in a car or on a bike) is ignored by design. Make sure location permission is enabled and you're moving at a walking or running pace.

My territory didn't get claimed.

A claim only registers when your route returns close to its starting point to close the loop. If the loop stays open, no area is enclosed. Make sure you finish near where you began.

Location permission

TURF needs location to track your route and center the map. "Always" lets a run keep tracking while your screen is locked; "While Using" works for foreground use. You can change this anytime in your device Settings → TURF → Location.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to the Profile tab, tap the gear (Settings) icon, scroll to Danger Zone, and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data. You can also email us to request deletion.

How is my data handled?

Your location is used only to run the game and is never sold or used for ads. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
