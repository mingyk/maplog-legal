Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

maplog ("we", "us", "the app") is a private, friends-only map you share with people you add. Photos you take are placed on a map at the spot where you took them and connected into the path of your day. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using maplog you agree to the practices described here and in our in-app Terms & Conditions.

Information We Collect

  • Location data. maplog uses your device location to place a photo on the map at the spot where you took it and to connect your photos into the path of your day. Location is only read while you are using the app — there is no background tracking.
  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive a unique identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email address. This is used to create and secure your account.
  • Content you create. The photos, captions, tags, and comments you post, along with the map locations they are placed at, are stored so your friends can see them for the short time before they are deleted.

How We Use Your Information

  • To place your photos on the map and connect them into your daily path.
  • To show your content to the friends you have added, and theirs to you.
  • To maintain your account and deliver notifications you have enabled.
  • To operate, secure, debug, and improve the app.

Your Content Disappears

maplog is ephemeral. Your shots and paths are automatically and permanently deleted a couple of days after you post them — there is no permanent archive inside the app. If you want to keep something, share it out (for example to your camera roll or Instagram) before it expires.

How We Share Information

Your location and personal data are not sold to third parties and are not used for advertising. Your profile and the content you post are visible only to people you have added as friends who have added you back. We use trusted infrastructure providers (such as our hosting and database provider) to store and process this data on our behalf.

Data Retention

Content you post is deleted automatically within a couple of days. You can permanently delete your entire account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Profile → Settings → Delete account), or by contacting us below.

Your Choices

  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data in the app at Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  • You can revoke location or photo access at any time in your device's Settings. Note that core features will not work without location.
  • You can request access to your data via the contact below.

Children

maplog is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Email us at mingyouk@gmail.com, or visit our Support page.
