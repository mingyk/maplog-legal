Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

TURF ("we", "us", "the app") is a fitness game that turns running and walking into territory you claim on a map. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using TURF you agree to the practices described here and in our in-app Terms & Conditions.

Information We Collect

  • Location data. TURF collects your device location while you record a run, and your approximate location while the app is open. This is used solely to provide the app's core features: tracking your route, calculating the territory you claim, centering the map on you, and displaying your position, your land, and leaderboards within the app.
  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple (or Google), we receive a unique identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email address. This is used to create and secure your account.
  • Activity data. Routes, distance, duration, and the territory you claim are stored so your progress, history, and leaderboard standing persist across sessions.

How We Use Your Information

  • To record runs and calculate the territory you claim.
  • To display your position, your land, other players' land, and leaderboards.
  • To maintain your account and keep your progress saved.
  • To operate, secure, debug, and improve the app.

How We Share Information

Your location and personal data are not sold to third parties and are not used for advertising. Your display name, profile color, and the territory you currently own are visible to other players within the app (for example on the map and leaderboards), which is core to how the game works. We use trusted infrastructure providers (such as our hosting and database provider) to store and process this data on our behalf.

Data Retention

We keep your account and activity data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Settings → Delete account), or by contacting us below.

Your Choices

  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data in the app at Settings → Delete account.
  • You can revoke location permission at any time in your device's Settings. Note that core features will not work without it.
  • You can request access to your data via the contact below.

Children

TURF is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Email us at mingyouk@gmail.com, or visit our Support page.
